Saved reports

A report is a dated copy of your portfolio and what it produced. It never changes when prices update, so you can compare it with today or with another report later. Reports are stored inside your packet.

    Compare

    Your packet

    A packet is a small .payout file holding your whole portfolio. It is your account: open it on any device to continue where you stopped.

    The file contains your holdings and share counts, not encrypted. Keep it somewhere private. Payout Lab never receives it.

    Links

    Workspace bookmark

    Opens Payout Lab on this browser and selects this portfolio. It contains no data and cannot bring your portfolio back on another device: there you open your packet.

    Snapshot link

    A dated copy of your holdings and settings stored inside the link. Anyone who has the link can read your holdings. It never updates: change your portfolio and you make a new link, while old links keep showing the old copy.

    Portfolio card

    A QR code with the name, ID and date, to save as an image or print. The default card is a bookmark with no holdings in it. A card that carries your holdings is a separate, deliberate choice.

    On this device

    Come back later

    A bookmark opens Payout Lab. On this browser it continues the portfolio it remembers; on another device you open your packet. If this site ever moves to a new web address, the copies remembered here do not move with it, so save a packet first.
